Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-12-27

This Privacy Policy explains how CollectorVault (the “App”) handles information. The App is designed to keep your collection on-device by default.

1) On-device data

  • Items, photos, attachments and notes are stored locally in the iOS sandbox.
  • Deleting the App removes local data from your device.

2) Photos, Camera, Face ID

  • Photos/Camera permissions are used to add images to your collection.
  • Face ID/Touch ID is used to protect access to the App.

3) AI identification

  • AI identification is optional. When used, a photo is sent to a Cloudflare proxy that calls Google Gemini.
  • The proxy does not store images or keep logs. The result is returned to your device.

4) Data we collect

  • No account required. No analytics, ads, or third‑party trackers.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple (StoreKit). We do not receive payment details.
